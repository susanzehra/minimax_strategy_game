Quick concept review

  • MAX chooses the largest available value.
  • MIN represents a rational opponent and chooses the smallest value.
  • Terminal nodes contain final utility scores.
  • Values are evaluated from the bottom upward.
A larger utility is better for MAX. A smaller utility is better for MIN.
