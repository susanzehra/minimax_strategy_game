Rules

  1. The game begins with seven power cells.
  2. You and the computer alternate turns.

  4. The player who takes the final cell wins.

Question 4 · Advanced Minimax Mission

Space Station Defense

You are the station commander (MAX). A cyberattacker (MIN) will always choose the response that hurts the station most. Work upward through the complete tree to find the defense with the best guaranteed outcome.

4 decision levels
24 terminal scores

Mission briefing

  1. Solve the tree from the bottom upward.
  2. At every MIN node, keep the smaller value.
  3. At every MAX node, keep the larger value.
  4. Complete each stage to unlock the next stage.
Utility scale: larger positive scores mean the station survives with more systems operating. Negative scores would favor the attacker.
